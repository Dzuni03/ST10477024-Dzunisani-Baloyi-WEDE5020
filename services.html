We as Shout SA focus on literacy because majority of South African children cannot read by the age of 12 which shows that most public schools dont have libraries. That means that when children cannot read they are not able to learn and therefore become dropouts which leads to crime that we as SHAOUT SA are trying to fix.
Children's education is important because it allows children a new direction in life as they can further their education and have a job indtead of commiting their to crime. With education they will be able to escape the poverty life and make something great for themselves and family as well as their community.
The community will be positively impacted because the children will have a safe place to study and do their homework. They will alos be occupied and will not abe running around the streets and picking up bad habits. The community will change to be a better place with educated children around.
