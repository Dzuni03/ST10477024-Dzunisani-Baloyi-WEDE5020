Education and Literacy Projects Across South Africa

SHOUT SA supports education and literacy throughout South Africa through initiatives that encourage reading, improve access to books and support school libraries. Explore our projects and discover how you can help make a difference.

Our History

SHOUT SA was founded in 2007 by Danny K and Kabelo Mabalane after the murder of Lucky Dube. It was established to encourage South Africans to act against crime The founders wanted to make a change starting with the children by providing them with resources they need to have a better education and increase their literacy.

Our Mission

We want to close the literacy gap by providing access to libraries, books and learning resources, creating a sustainable social impact through education.

Our Team

Our Vision

We want to create a better South Africa by empowering children through education and giving the children an opportunity to learn and grow.
